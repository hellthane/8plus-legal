Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 15, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Rodrigo Vrinda ("we," "our," or "us") uses your information when you use +8 Alarm.

2. Information Use

We do not collect personal identification information. However, the app requires specific permissions to function correctly and provide its core features.

3. Microphone Access (RECORD_AUDIO)

Our app requires access to your device's microphone.

  • Purpose: This permission is used solely to record a voice message at your explicit request to be used as an alarm or notification sound.
  • Handling: Audio data is processed locally on your device. No audio data is ever transmitted to any server or third party.
  • Storage: We do not store your audio recordings on any remote cloud. We never share your audio data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Safety

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. Your data stays on your device under your control.
